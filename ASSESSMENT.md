## Part 2 – Business Understanding

### What is Caprae's Mission?
Caprae Capital's mission is to transform good businesses into great ones through strategic, hands-on value creation rather than financial engineering alone. Where many PE firms lean on leverage and cost-cutting, Caprae positions itself as a founder/operator-first firm that invests in the operational and technological upside of the companies it acquires.

Central to this is treating M&A as a seven-year journey: the greater value is created after the acquisition, not at the signing. Through its SaaS and "M&A as a Service" models, Caprae supports businesses post-acquisition with practical tools, including AI, to improve decision-making, streamline operations and unlock growth.

Caprae also builds its own tooling, such as the SaaSquatch lead generation platform, instead of renting someone else's. The mission is therefore as much about culture and talent (character, courage, creativity) as it is about capital: finding high-horsepower people and giving them the platform to build something lasting.
